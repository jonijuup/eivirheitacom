# Ei virheitä

## Audience

Listeners of the Ei virheitä podcast ("No mistakes"). The intro plays before each episode.

## What it shows

- A CRT screen fading in from black, a caret blinking on the beat as the music starts.
- The show's name typed with mistakes and corrected by hand: "Ei vireitä", "Ei vireihtä", "Ei virheitä", "Ei virheitä podcats", "Ei virheitä podcast."
- The full stop lands on the track's hit, then the screen fades to black.

## Facts and sources

The copy is the show's name, as given. No other claims.

## Length, format, tone

14.8 s (one play of Digital Dawn), 1920×1080, 60 fps. Retro, dry, a little funny: the joke is the typing.

## Sound

- `audio/digital-dawn.wav`: the track (120 BPM, bass out 7.2 to 9.4 s, near silence 10.4 to 12.6 s, hit at 12.78 s).
- `audio/sfx/keyboard-typing.wav`: soft typing, cut into single keystrokes by `mix.ts`.
- `audio/mix/no-mistakes.wav`: what the video plays, built by `node videos/no-mistakes/mix.ts` from `script.ts`. Rebuild it after changing the typing.
